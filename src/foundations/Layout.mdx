# Layout

- **Grid.** All slide templates carry a layout grid — use it to keep content aligned across slides with consistent spacing and proportions (toggle in Figma with Shift + G). 12 columns on the 1920×1080 canvas with a `--margin-x` (18–20px) outer margin.
- **Breathing room.** Content always gets enough white space. Stick to the grid and deliberately keep areas of the slide empty.
- **Content layouts.** Editorial (text + image), simple, and text only.
- **Section title.** Structures long presentations — use the space to give a rundown of what follows.
- **Presentation cover.** A client cover prominently features one representative image — it's the slide that stays as the thumbnail.
- **Image grids.** Show one or a few images prominently filling the space, or many in a grid.
- **Deck header.** The chapter bar sits 48px tall across the top: tab · deck title · ©year Above confidential.

## Imagery

Studio staged · cold warmth · natural · unexpected combinations · process · culture (documentary). Renders: product rendering, detail shots, in-context renderings, and artistic hero shots that capture the feeling rather than document the design.
