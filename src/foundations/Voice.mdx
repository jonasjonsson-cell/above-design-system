# Voice & content

How ABOVE writes. Terse, spoken, facilitative — copy reads like a workshop agenda said out loud.

| Principle | In practice |
| --- | --- |
| Fragments over sentences | "Show & tell of project, pitch, tool or inspiration." · "Round the table." |
| Sentence case, almost always | "What's up?", "Collaborative session". The only uppercase is the mono header label — a typographic choice, not emphasis. |
| Direct address | The studio talks to its own team: "What's up?", "Reflections?", "Brainpower of all". |
| Numerals as graphics | Durations ("30 min") and indices ("01") are content *and* ornament, set at poster scale. |
| Bilingual warmth | Swedish at human moments — the closing slide says "Tack!", not "Thank you". |
| Minimal punctuation | Curly apostrophes, the middot `·` as separator ("AI · Motion · Culture"), colons to label ("Well-being: temp check"). |
| No emoji as UI | A glyph like ✌ appears once, huge, as typographic illustration — never inline or as bullets. |

**Do:** one idea per slide · sentence case · fragments · numerals at scale · the occasional Swedish word.

**Don't:** title-case headlines · paragraphs of body copy · emoji bullets · exclamation marks everywhere · filler subtitles.
